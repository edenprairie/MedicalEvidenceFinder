# Skill Workshop Demo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Calm technical product walkthrough for business and engineering leaders, using the MedicalEvidenceFinder interface language: deep green canvas, warm paper panels, pale green borders, amber highlights for review decisions, and clear editorial typography.

## Colors

- #123c38 deep green background
- #f4f5ef warm paper canvas
- #ffffff content panels
- #d7a13b review amber
- #23705d active green

## Typography

Use a system sans-serif stack with a monospace style for version and registry details.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not imply that markdown editing retrains an LLM.
- Do not show a skill becoming active without approval.
- Do not present synthetic evidence as clinical evidence.
- Do not hide the rollback path.
